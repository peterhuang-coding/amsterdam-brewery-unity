## Explicit ferry and save behavior

The GVB F3 Centraal–Buiksloterweg crossing is an explicit transit leg between the two verified ground anchors. NAV never calls IJ water walkable. `City.route` combines safe ground legs with an explicitly tagged ferry leg only when its boarding route and onward route are valid. No straight ground shortcut across water. The ferry leg has canonical IDs/from/to and finite progress, and cannot start away from its boarding point.

City state includes a map version and, while aboard, a validated transit state. Position during transit is derived from its canonical endpoints/progress. Carrying a bicycle persists; a distant parked bicycle does not teleport aboard. Cancelling a route or pressing movement keys aboard cancels onward intent but continues the boat to the destination bank. Pausing stops simulation; refresh restores the same crossing, and subsequent ticks reach safe ground even if the ephemeral route array is empty. Malformed transit resets safely to a valid bank/pub while retaining valid visit history. No save may restore an ordinary walker onto water.

Old `ab_reopening_v1` game/economy data remains intact. Old City coordinates have no new map-version tag and must be migrated once: relocate player and independently parked bicycle to corresponding safe named anchors where known, otherwise the pub; retain valid visited-place IDs. Do not reinterpret old 1800×1120 coordinates as metres in the new city. New saves validate their version, finite coordinates, walkability and bicycle/transit consistency, clone values, and do not mutate inputs. The browser integration clears incompatible old ephemeral route/cart positions and presents a short migration notice.
